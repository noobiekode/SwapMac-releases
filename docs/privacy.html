Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

In short: SwapMac looks at your files only to measure and explain them, on your device. Your scans never leave it. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The only things that go over the internet are update checks, license checks and purchases, described below.

What SwapMac reads

SwapMac reads the names, sizes and dates of files and folders, on your Mac or in folders you pick on iPhone and iPad, so it can show what's using space and explain what each item is. On a Mac it also reads live system figures such as CPU, memory, network and battery use. All of this stays on your device. SwapMac doesn't read the contents of your documents, and it doesn't send file names, folder contents, scan results or system figures anywhere.

What SwapMac keeps on your device

  • Your settings, such as theme, menu bar and shortcut choices.
  • Cleanup History: the names, sizes and original locations of items you moved to the Trash with SwapMac, so it can put them back.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the folders you picked, so you can scan them again.
  • For the direct-download Mac app, when your Pro trial started and, after you activate Pro, your license key.

To remove them, delete SwapMac's folder in Library/Application Support and its settings file in Library/Preferences (on iPhone and iPad, delete the app).

What goes over the internet

  • Update checks (Mac, direct download). SwapMac downloads a list of available versions from GitHub. Like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address, and the request names SwapMac and its version.
  • License activation and checks (Mac, direct download). When you enter a license key, SwapMac sends the key and your Mac's model (for example "MacBookPro18,3") to Dodo Payments to activate it, and checks the key with Dodo Payments about once a week. It doesn't send your name, email or your Mac's name.
  • Buying Pro (Mac, direct download). Checkout happens on Dodo Payments' website. Dodo Payments is the seller of record: it handles your payment details, billing address, receipt email and tax under its own privacy policy. SwapMac never sees your card details.
  • In-app purchases (Mac App Store, iPhone and iPad). Apple handles payment. SwapMac uses RevenueCat to confirm what you've bought; RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for the app and your purchase records from Apple, under its privacy policy. Nothing that identifies you personally is sent.

Crash reports and feedback

After a crash, the direct-download Mac app may offer to email the crash report that macOS saved. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it, and you see the email first. The report holds technical details such as your macOS version, SwapMac's version and the code that was running. Feedback opens an email in your own mail app, which you can read and change before sending. It contains what you write and, unless you switch them off, technical details shown to you first: SwapMac's version, your system version, your device's chip or model and memory, whether Full Disk Access is on, your Pro status, and a summary of your last scan (its kind, size and counts, never file names). A report about one item also includes where that item is, with your home folder shown as ~. App Store versions don't do this; Apple shares crash reports with developers only if you've allowed it in your device's settings.

Full Disk Access

On a Mac, SwapMac asks for Full Disk Access so it can measure folders macOS otherwise hides, such as Mail and Messages. It uses that access only to read sizes and names for the scan, never to send anything.

Children

SwapMac isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
