What's new in SwapMac 0.2.0

  • Dashboard: a new first screen with your Mac's storage, memory, battery and what's safe to clear, at a glance.
  • A clearer cleanup: every item says what happens if you clear it, and afterwards a summary shows exactly what moved, what was skipped and why.
  • Cleanup History: grouped by cleanup, with the date each item leaves the Trash. Put Back never overwrites anything, and moving one item offers Undo.
  • A truthful uninstaller: finds an app's helpers, plug-ins and system files too, lets you keep any part, asks for your password once, and says honestly what's left.
  • Calmer setup: SwapMac explains before it asks for access, scans read-only, and shows which folders it couldn't read and why.
  • Where the used space is: what you can review, what macOS manages itself (snapshots, swap, the system) and what couldn't be read.
  • Settings in tabs, a Privacy tab listing exactly what SwapMac sends, clear Pro terms, and SwapMac → Uninstall SwapMac… to remove it completely.

Nothing changes until you choose it, and everything still goes to the Trash first.
