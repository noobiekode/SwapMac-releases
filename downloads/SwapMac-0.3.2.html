What's new in SwapMac 0.3.2

  • Full Disk Access stays on after updates: until now, macOS turned it off with every update. Please turn it on one last time: in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access, remove SwapMac with the − button, add it again with +, then reopen SwapMac. From this version on, it stays on.
  • Fixed: after reopening SwapMac without Full Disk Access, the window could get stuck with an empty sidebar and the top of the page out of reach.
