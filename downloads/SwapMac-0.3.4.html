What's new in SwapMac 0.3.4

  • Fits a half-screen window: snap SwapMac to one side of the screen and Details closes by itself, so the page keeps its width. Select something and it opens again.
  • Details only where it's useful: it appears on the Dashboard, Explained, Map, Large Files and search, and no longer takes room on screens with nothing to select.
  • Cleaner narrow layouts: the Overview and Dashboard cards wrap and stack instead of squeezing their numbers.
