What's new in SwapMac 0.2.1

  • Quieter in the background: with its window closed, SwapMac now uses no CPU at all. Live numbers refresh only while you can see them, and more slowly on battery and in Low Power Mode.
  • Activity: a new screen listing cleanups, uninstalls, put-backs, items about to leave the Trash, and updates, each with one thing you can do about it.
  • Settings → Monitoring: turn live numbers off, choose how often they refresh, see what SwapMac itself uses, and choose notifications. They're off unless you turn them on, and never about buying anything.
  • Optional low-space alert when free space drops below 10 GB, at most once a day.
